# Privacy Policy for Kalkulacka

**Last Updated: June 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Kalkulacka ("App," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our iOS application.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Health Information

When you use Kalkulacka, we collect the following personal health information to provide core functionality:

- **Profile Data**: Age, gender, weight, height, and BMI
- **Consumption Tracking**: Records of alcohol and caffeine consumption, including:
  - Type and name of drinks consumed
  - Volume and alcohol percentage (for alcohol)
  - Caffeine content (for caffeine beverages)
  - Timestamp of each entry
  - Calculated Blood Alcohol Content (BAC)

### 2.2 Device Information

We automatically collect certain information about your device, including:

- Device type and operating system
- App version
- Basic usage analytics (optional)

### 2.3 Information You Provide

All personal information is provided voluntarily by you through:
- User profile setup
- Drink entry forms
- Manual drink additions

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

### 3.1 Core App Functionality

- **BAC Calculation**: Your personal health data (age, gender, weight, height) is used exclusively to calculate your Blood Alcohol Content using the Widmark formula
- **Tracking**: Recording and displaying your daily alcohol and caffeine consumption
- **Progress Monitoring**: Showing your progress toward daily limits (4 standard drinks for alcohol, 400mg for caffeine)
- **Health Insights**: Providing personalized health information based on your data

### 3.2 App Improvement

- Analyzing app performance and stability
- Understanding user engagement patterns
- Improving app features and user experience

### 3.3 No Marketing or Advertising

We do **not** use your personal health information for marketing, advertising, or profiling purposes.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Local Storage

- Your personal profile information and drink history are primarily stored locally on your device using iOS secure storage mechanisms
- This data remains under your control and on your device

### 4.2 Cloud Storage (Optional)

- The App uses Convex backend services to optionally sync your data across devices
- All data transmission uses industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/SSL)
- Your data is stored securely on Convex's servers with encryption at rest

### 4.3 Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including:

- Data encryption in transit (TLS/SSL)
- Secure authentication mechanisms
- Regular security assessments

## 5. Data Sharing

### 5.1 Third-Party Sharing

We **do not sell, rent, or share** your personal health information with third parties except as required by law.

### 5.2 Service Providers

We may share information with service providers who assist us in operating the App and providing services to you:

- **Convex**: Backend database and sync services
- These service providers are contractually obligated to use your information only as necessary to provide services to us

### 5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or if we believe in good faith that such disclosure is necessary to:

- Comply with legal obligations or law enforcement requests
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Protect the safety, rights, or property of users or the public

## 6. Your Privacy Rights

### 6.1 Access and Control

You have the right to:

- Access your personal data within the App
- Edit your profile information at any time
- Delete your personal data by clearing app data or deleting the App

### 6.2 Data Deletion

- **Local Deletion**: You can delete individual drink entries or clear all app data directly from the App
- **Complete Removal**: Uninstalling the App will remove local data from your device
- **Cloud Data**: If using Convex sync, you can request deletion of data from our servers

### 6.3 Data Portability

You can export your data by:
- Taking screenshots of your information
- Requesting your data in a structured format by contacting us

## 7. Children's Privacy

Kalkulacka is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 18, we will delete such information promptly.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data as follows:

- **Local Data**: Retained on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App
- **Cloud Data**: Retained on Convex servers for the duration of your use; deleted upon request
- **Usage Analytics**: Automatically deleted after 90 days (if enabled)
